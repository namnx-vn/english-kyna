# PART 1 - What do you usually do after work?

## 1. Core question

### Main question

> **What do you usually do after work?**  
> Bạn thường làm gì sau giờ làm?

A simple answer:

> I usually go home and have dinner with my family.

A better answer:

> I usually go home after work and have dinner with my family. After that, I relax for a while, listen to music, or watch something online.

A more natural answer:

> After work, I usually go home and spend some time with my family. If I'm not too tired, I go for a walk or listen to music. It helps me relax after a long day.

### Structure

```text
After work, I usually + VERB ...
I usually + VERB ... after work.
```

Examples:

- After work, I usually **go home**.
- I usually **have dinner with my family** after work.
- I usually **listen to music** in the evening.
- Sometimes I **go for a walk** after dinner.

## 3. Adverbs of frequency

These words are very important because the question contains **usually**.

| Word | Meaning | Approx. frequency |
|---|---|---:|
| always | luôn luôn | 100% |
| usually | thường | 80% |
| often | thường xuyên | 60-70% |
| sometimes | thỉnh thoảng | 40-50% |
| occasionally | đôi khi | 20-30% |
| rarely / seldom | hiếm khi | 10% |
| never | không bao giờ | 0% |

### Position in a sentence

Usually before the main verb:

```text
I usually go home at 6 p.m.
I often listen to music.
I sometimes exercise after work.
I rarely meet friends on weekdays.
```

But after the verb **be**:

```text
I am usually tired after work.
I am sometimes busy in the evening.
```

Remember:

```text
Subject + adverb + main verb
Subject + be + adverb
```

## 4. Present Simple - grammar you need for this lesson

We use the **Present Simple** for routines and repeated activities.

### Positive

```text
I/You/We/They + V
He/She/It + V-s/es
```

Examples:

- I **go** home at 6 p.m.
- My wife **gets** home later than me.
- He **watches** TV after dinner.

### Negative

```text
I/You/We/They + don't + V
He/She/It + doesn't + V
```

Examples:

- I don't usually go out after work.
- He doesn't watch TV every day.

### Question

```text
Do + I/you/we/they + V ...?
Does + he/she/it + V ...?
```

Examples:

- Do you exercise after work?
- Do you usually have dinner at home?
- Does your wife get home early?

Important:

```text
Does + subject + base verb
```

❌ Does he goes to the gym?  
✅ Does he go to the gym?

## 6. Connectors to make my answer longer

Avoid giving only one sentence. Connect ideas with:

- **First, ...**
- **Then, ...**
- **After that, ...**
- **Later, ...**
- **Finally, ...**
- **Usually, ...**
- **Sometimes, ...**
- **If I have time, ...**
- **If I'm not too tired, ...**
- **because ...**
- **so ...**

Example:

> After work, I usually go home first. Then I have dinner with my family. After that, I may listen to music or go for a short walk. If I'm tired, I just stay home and relax.

## 7. Sentence patterns to memorize

### Talking about a routine

```text
After work, I usually + V.
```

> After work, I usually go home and have dinner.

### Adding frequency

```text
I often/sometimes/rarely + V.
```

> I sometimes go for a walk after dinner.

### Adding a condition

```text
If I have time, I + V.
If I'm not too tired, I + V.
```

> If I have time, I study English for about 30 minutes.

### Giving a reason

```text
I + V + because + reason.
```

> I listen to music because it helps me relax.

### Talking about time

```text
I usually + V + at around + TIME.
```

> I usually get home at around 6:30 p.m.

### Talking about frequency per week

```text
I + V + once/twice/three times a week.
```

> I study English three times a week.

## 9. Model answers

### 20-second answer

> After work, I usually go home and have dinner with my family. After that, I relax, listen to music, or watch something online. If I'm not too tired, I sometimes go for a walk.

### 40-second answer

> I usually finish work in the evening and go home. First, I have dinner with my family and take a short rest. After that, I usually listen to music or spend some time learning English. Sometimes I go for a walk if I'm not too tired. I like having a quiet evening because it helps me relax after work.

### Natural conversational version

> It depends on the day. Most of the time, I just go home, have dinner, and spend time with my family. Sometimes I listen to music or walk around for a bit. If I have an English lesson, I try to prepare before class.

Useful phrase:

> **It depends on the day.**  
> Tùy từng ngày.

## 11. `preserve` vs `protect` vs `promote`

These three words are easy to confuse.

### PRESERVE = bảo tồn / giữ gìn

Use it when you want something to **continue to exist in its original or traditional form**.

```text
preserve + culture/tradition/heritage/custom
```

Examples:

> We should preserve our traditional culture.

> The festival helps preserve local traditions.

> We need to preserve these customs for future generations.

### PROTECT = bảo vệ khỏi nguy hiểm / tổn hại

```text
protect + something + from + danger/problem
```

Examples:

> We need to protect historic buildings from damage.

> The government should protect cultural heritage sites.

### PROMOTE = quảng bá / thúc đẩy

Use it when you want **more people to know about or support something**.

```text
promote + culture/tourism/event/product
promote + something + to + people
```

Examples:

> Social media can help promote the festival.

> The event promotes Vietnamese culture to international visitors.

### Quick memory trick

```text
PRESERVE -> keep it alive
PROTECT  -> keep it safe
PROMOTE  -> make it known
```

## 12. Questions about an event or festival

### What time does it begin?

```text
What time + does + it/the event/the festival + begin?
```

> What time does the festival begin?

> It begins at 8 a.m.

Important:

❌ What time does the festival begins?  
✅ What time does the festival begin?

Because after **does**, use the base verb.

### What time does it end?

> What time does the event end?

> It ends at around 10 p.m.

### When does it take place?

> When does the festival take place?

> It takes place in January / during Tet / every spring.

### Where is it held?

> Where is the festival held?

> It is held in Hanoi.

### How long does it last?

> How long does the festival last?

> It lasts for three days.

### What do people do there?

> People watch traditional performances, eat local food, and take part in different activities.

### Why is it important?

> It is important because it helps preserve our culture and brings people together.

## 13. Active vs passive structures

### Active

```text
People hold the festival every year.
```

### Passive - more natural when talking about events

```text
The festival is held every year.
```

Useful patterns:

```text
The event is held in + PLACE.
The festival is celebrated in + MONTH/SEASON.
Traditional food is served during the festival.
```

Examples:

> The festival is held in my hometown every year.

> Traditional dishes are served during the celebration.

For Beginner level, memorize the whole chunks first instead of worrying too much about the grammar name.

---

## 14. Talking about purpose

Use **to + verb** to explain purpose.

```text
The festival is organized to + V.
People celebrate it to + V.
```

Examples:

> The festival is organized to promote local culture.

> People celebrate it to remember their ancestors.

> The event helps to preserve traditional customs.

Another useful structure:

```text
The purpose of the festival is to + V.
```

> The purpose of the festival is to preserve local traditions and promote tourism.

---

## 15. Giving opinions and suggestions

### Opinion

```text
I think + sentence.
In my opinion, + sentence.
For me, + sentence.
```

Examples:

> I think traditional festivals are important.

> In my opinion, young people should learn more about local traditions.

### Suggestion

```text
We should + V.
We can + V.
People can + V.
```

Examples:

> We should preserve traditional customs.

> We can use social media to promote the festival.

> The government should protect important cultural sites.

## 16. Festival answer framework - 5W1H

If I don't know what to say, use this order:

```text
WHAT  -> What festival/event is it?
WHEN  -> When does it take place?
WHERE -> Where is it held?
WHO   -> Who celebrates/joins it?
WHAT  -> What do people do?
WHY   -> Why is it important / why do you like it?
```

Example:

> Tet is the biggest traditional festival in Vietnam. It usually takes place around January or February. People celebrate it all over the country. Families get together, prepare traditional food, visit relatives, and wish each other good luck. I think Tet is important because it helps preserve Vietnamese traditions and brings families together.

# PART 3 - Common mistakes

## 18. Mistakes to avoid

### 1. Forgetting `do` in a question

❌ What you usually do after work?  
✅ What **do** you usually do after work?

### 2. Using `-s` after `does`

❌ What time does it begins?  
✅ What time does it **begin**?

### 3. Wrong frequency word position

❌ I go usually home at 6.  
✅ I **usually go** home at 6.

### 4. `go to home`

❌ I go to home after work.  
✅ I go home after work.

### 5. `relax myself`

Usually unnecessary:

❌ I relax myself after work.  
✅ I relax after work.

### 6. `listen music`

❌ I listen music.  
✅ I listen **to** music.

### 7. Confusing preserve / protect / promote

```text
preserve culture
protect culture/sites from damage
promote culture/tourism/event
```

# PART 5 - Speaking drills

## 20. Drill 1 - Change only one part

Start with:

> After work, I usually go home.

Change the activity:

> After work, I usually have dinner with my family.

> After work, I usually listen to music.

> After work, I usually study English.

> After work, I usually go for a walk.

Then change frequency:

> I always go home after work.

> I often listen to music after work.

> I sometimes go for a walk after work.

> I rarely meet friends after work.

---

## 21. Drill 2 - Answer in 3 sentences

For every question, use:

```text
Sentence 1: direct answer
Sentence 2: detail
Sentence 3: reason / extra information
```

Example:

**What do you usually do after work?**

> I usually go home and have dinner with my family. After that, I listen to music or study English. I prefer quiet evenings because they help me relax.

---

## 22. Drill 3 - Festival mini answer

> __________ is a popular festival in Vietnam. It takes place __________. People usually __________. I think it is important because __________.

Then extend:

> It is held in __________. It usually begins at __________ and lasts for __________. The festival helps preserve __________ and promote __________.

