## 2. The 3 important words: preserve / protect / promote

### PRESERVE = bảo tồn, giữ cho một thứ tiếp tục tồn tại

Use with:

```text
preserve culture
preserve traditions
preserve customs
preserve cultural heritage
preserve historical buildings
```

Examples:

> We should preserve our traditional culture.

> The festival helps preserve local traditions.

> It is important to preserve these customs for future generations.

### PROTECT = bảo vệ khỏi nguy hiểm, hư hại hoặc mất mát

Useful pattern:

```text
protect + something
protect + something + from + danger/problem
```

Examples:

> We need to protect historical buildings.

> We should protect cultural heritage sites from damage.

> Local people work together to protect the environment around the festival area.

### PROMOTE = quảng bá / giúp nhiều người biết đến hơn

Use with:

```text
promote culture
promote tourism
promote an event
promote local products
```

Examples:

> Social media can help promote the festival.

> The event promotes Vietnamese culture to international visitors.

> The festival also helps promote local tourism.

### Easy memory trick

```text
PRESERVE -> keep it alive
PROTECT  -> keep it safe
PROMOTE  -> make it known
```

## 3. Asking about an event

These are the most important question patterns to memorize.

### A. What time does it begin?

```text
What time + does + EVENT + begin?
```

> What time does the festival begin?

> It begins at 8 a.m.

Important:

```text
does + begin
```

NOT:

```text
does + begins
```

### B. What time does it end?

> What time does the event end?

> It ends at around 10 p.m.

### C. When does it take place?

> When does the festival take place?

Possible answers:

> It takes place every year.

> It takes place in spring.

> It takes place during Tet holiday.

### D. Where is it held?

> Where is the festival held?

> It is held in Hanoi.

> It is held in my hometown.

### E. How long does it last?

> How long does the festival last?

> It lasts for two days.

> It lasts for about three hours.

### F. How often is it held?

> How often is the festival held?

> It is held once a year.

> It takes place every year.

### G. Who organizes it?

> Who organizes the event?

> It is organized by the local government.

or simply:

> The local government organizes it.

### H. What do people do there?

> What do people do at the festival?

> People watch traditional performances, eat local food, and take part in different activities.

### I. Why is it important?

> Why is the festival important?

> It is important because it helps preserve our culture and brings people together.

### J. Why do people celebrate it?

> People celebrate it to remember an important tradition and spend time together.

## 4. Time expressions: at / on / in

### AT + exact time

```text
at 8 a.m.
at 7:30 p.m.
at noon
```

> The ceremony begins at 8 a.m.

### ON + day / date

```text
on Monday
on October 2nd
on the first day of Tet
```

> The main event is held on Sunday.

### IN + month / year / season

```text
in January
in 2026
in spring
in the evening
```

> The festival usually takes place in spring.

### DURING + event / period

```text
during Tet
during the festival
during the holiday
```

> People visit their relatives during Tet.

## 5. Present Simple for schedules

Use the Present Simple when talking about regular events or official schedules.

```text
The event begins at 8 a.m.
The festival ends at 10 p.m.
It takes place every year.
The celebration lasts for three days.
```

Question:

```text
What time does it begin?
When does it take place?
How long does it last?
```

Remember:

```text
DOES + subject + BASE VERB
```

Examples:

> When does the event start?

> How long does the ceremony last?

## 6. Passive voice chunks to memorize

For this level, memorize these as complete phrases.

```text
The event is held in + PLACE.
The festival is celebrated every year.
The event is organized by + PERSON/ORGANIZATION.
Traditional food is served during the festival.
Traditional costumes are worn by local people.
```

Examples:

> The festival is held in my hometown every year.

> It is organized by the local community.

> Traditional food is served during the celebration.

### Active vs passive

```text
People hold the festival every year.
The festival is held every year.
```

Both are correct, but the passive form is very common when describing events.

## 7. Useful sentence structures

### Describe when an event happens

```text
It takes place in + MONTH/SEASON.
It takes place every + TIME PERIOD.
```

> It takes place every spring.

### Describe the venue

```text
It is held in/at + PLACE.
```

> It is held in Hanoi.

> It is held at the city square.

### Describe duration

```text
It lasts for + LENGTH OF TIME.
```

> It lasts for three days.

### Describe activities

```text
People usually + V1, V2, and V3.
```

> People usually watch performances, eat traditional food, and visit local temples.

### Explain purpose

```text
It is organized to + VERB.
People celebrate it to + VERB.
```

> It is organized to preserve local traditions.

> People celebrate it to remember their ancestors.

### Explain importance

```text
It is important because + CLAUSE.
```

> It is important because it helps preserve our culture.

### Add another benefit

```text
It also helps + VERB.
```

> It also helps promote local tourism.

## 10. A reusable 5W1H speaking framework

When you do not know what to say, use:

```text
WHAT  -> What is the event?
WHEN  -> When does it happen?
WHERE -> Where is it held?
WHO   -> Who joins / organizes it?
WHAT  -> What do people do?
WHY   -> Why is it important?
```

Example:

> Tet is the most important traditional holiday in Vietnam. It usually takes place in January or February. Families celebrate it all over the country. People clean and decorate their homes, prepare traditional food, and visit relatives. I think it is important because it brings families together and helps preserve Vietnamese traditions.

## 11. Model answer - Tet Festival

### Short version - 20 to 30 seconds

> My favorite festival is Tet, or Lunar New Year. It usually takes place in January or February. During Tet, people spend time with their families, eat traditional food, and visit relatives. I like it because it brings families together.

### Better version - 40 to 60 seconds

> One of the most important festivals in Vietnam is Tet, or Lunar New Year. It usually takes place in January or February and is celebrated throughout the country. Before Tet, people clean and decorate their homes and prepare traditional food. During the holiday, families visit relatives, give lucky money to children, and spend time together. I think Tet is important because it helps preserve Vietnamese traditions and brings families closer together.

### Stronger version with T08 vocabulary

> Tet is an important part of Vietnamese cultural heritage. It is celebrated every year throughout Vietnam. People prepare traditional food, visit relatives, and take part in many traditional activities. I think we should preserve these customs for future generations. We can also promote Vietnamese culture by introducing Tet to international visitors.

## 12. Model answer - a local festival

Use this template for any festival:

> The festival I want to talk about is __________. It takes place in __________ and is held in __________. It usually lasts for __________. During the festival, people __________, __________, and __________. It is important because __________. I think we should preserve it because __________.

Example:

> The festival I want to talk about is a traditional festival in my hometown. It takes place every spring and is held near the local temple. It usually lasts for two days. During the festival, people watch traditional performances, pray for good luck, and eat local food. It is important because it helps preserve local culture and brings the community together.

**You:** I think they are important because they help preserve local culture and traditions.

**Teacher:** How can we promote them?

**You:** We can promote them through social media and tourism websites. We can also introduce them to international visitors.

## 14. Common mistakes

### Mistake 1

❌ What time does it begins?

✅ What time does it begin?

Reason:

```text
does + base verb
```

### Mistake 2

❌ The festival hold every year.

✅ The festival is held every year.

or

✅ People hold the festival every year.

### Mistake 3

❌ It lasts three days.

✅ It lasts for three days.

### Mistake 4

❌ It takes place at January.

✅ It takes place in January.

### Mistake 5

❌ It is held at Vietnam.

✅ It is held in Vietnam.

### Mistake 6

❌ We should promote our tradition for future generations.

Better:

✅ We should preserve our traditions for future generations.

Use **promote** when you mean making more people aware of something.
