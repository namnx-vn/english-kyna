# Session 2 - Speaking Practice: Talk about a Festival

> Practice topic: **Festivals / Celebrations**  
> Main goal: Speak for 1-2 minutes about a familiar festival without translating sentence by sentence from Vietnamese.  
> Suggested example: **Tet / Lunar New Year in Vietnam**

---

## 1. Speaking structure to remember

When talking about a festival, use this simple order:

```text
1. What festival is it?
2. When is it celebrated?
3. Why is it important?
4. What do people do before the festival?
5. What do people do during the festival?
6. What food / decorations are common?
7. What do I usually do?
8. Why do I like it?
```

You do not need difficult vocabulary. The priority is to speak clearly and continuously.

## 5. Important sentence patterns

### A. Introducing the festival

> My favorite festival is **Tet**, also known as **Lunar New Year**.

> I would like to talk about **Tet**, which is the most important traditional festival in Vietnam.

Easy version:

> I want to talk about Tet. It is a very important festival in Vietnam.

---

### B. Saying when it happens

> Tet usually takes place in **January or February**, depending on the lunar calendar.

> It is celebrated at the beginning of the lunar year.

Beginner version:

> Tet is usually in January or February.

---

### C. Saying why it is important

> It is important because it is a time for family reunions.

> It gives family members a chance to spend time together.

> For many Vietnamese people, Tet represents a new beginning.

Useful pattern:

```text
It is important because + sentence.
```

---

### D. Talking about preparation

> Before Tet, people clean and decorate their houses.

> Many families buy flowers, food, and new clothes.

> People often prepare traditional dishes for their families.

Useful pattern:

```text
Before + festival/event, people usually + verb.
```

---

### E. Talking about activities

> During Tet, people visit their relatives and friends.

> Children usually receive lucky money from adults.

> Families eat traditional food and spend time together.

Useful pattern:

```text
During Tet, people usually + verb.
```

---

### F. Giving your opinion

> My favorite part of Tet is spending time with my family.

> I like Tet because everyone can relax and be together.

> For me, Tet is special because it reminds me of my family and my childhood.

Useful patterns:

```text
My favorite part of ... is ...
I like ... because ...
For me, ... is special because ...
```

## 6. Grammar focus for speaking

### Present Simple - for traditions and habits

Use the Present Simple because you are talking about things that usually happen.

```text
People clean their houses.
Families visit their relatives.
Children receive lucky money.
We have dinner together.
```

### Adverbs of frequency

```text
always
usually
often
sometimes
```

Examples:

> We **usually** visit my grandparents on the first day of Tet.

> I **often** meet my relatives during the holiday.

### `because` - give a reason

Do not stop at:

> I like Tet.

Expand it:

> I like Tet **because I can spend more time with my family**.

## 7. 30-second easy answer

Use this version if you feel nervous.

> My favorite festival is **Tet**. It is the Lunar New Year in Vietnam. It usually happens in January or February. Before Tet, people clean and decorate their houses. During Tet, families spend time together, visit relatives, and eat traditional food. Children also receive lucky money. I like Tet because I can relax and spend time with my family.

### Speaking tip

Do not try to say it very fast. Pause naturally after each idea:

```text
My favorite festival is Tet. /
It is the Lunar New Year in Vietnam. /
It usually happens in January or February. /
Before Tet, ...
```

---

## 8. 60-second natural answer

> One of my favorite festivals is **Tet, or Lunar New Year**. It is probably the most important traditional festival in Vietnam, and it usually takes place in January or February.
>
> Before Tet, people are very busy preparing for the holiday. They clean and decorate their houses, buy flowers, prepare traditional food, and sometimes buy new clothes.
>
> During Tet, families get together, have meals, visit relatives, and wish each other good health and good luck. Children usually receive lucky money in red envelopes.
>
> Personally, my favorite part of Tet is spending time with my family. Everyone is normally busy with work, so Tet is a good opportunity for us to relax, talk, and enjoy time together.

Do not memorize every word. Memorize the **idea order**.

---

## 9. 90-second answer - better for speaking practice

> I would like to talk about **Tet, the Vietnamese Lunar New Year**. It is the biggest traditional holiday in Vietnam and usually falls in January or February, depending on the lunar calendar.
>
> For Vietnamese people, Tet is not only a holiday but also a time for family reunions and new beginnings. Before Tet, people usually clean their houses, decorate them with flowers, buy food, and prepare traditional dishes such as banh chung. The streets and shops are often very busy and colorful during this period.
>
> During Tet, family members spend a lot of time together. We visit grandparents and relatives, have family meals, and give each other New Year wishes. Adults normally give lucky money to children in red envelopes. Some people also go to temples or watch fireworks.
>
> For me, the best thing about Tet is the family atmosphere. In daily life, everyone can be busy with work, so Tet gives us a chance to slow down and reconnect. That is why Tet is very meaningful to me.

## 11. Mini dialogue with the teacher

### Basic version

**Teacher:** What is your favorite festival?  
**Me:** My favorite festival is Tet, or Lunar New Year.

**Teacher:** When is it celebrated?  
**Me:** It is usually celebrated in January or February.

**Teacher:** What do people do before Tet?  
**Me:** They usually clean and decorate their houses and prepare traditional food.

**Teacher:** What do you usually do during Tet?  
**Me:** I spend time with my family, visit relatives, and have family meals.

**Teacher:** Why do you like Tet?  
**Me:** I like it because I can relax and spend more time with my family.

---

### Follow-up version

**Teacher:** Is Tet different now compared with the past?  
**Me:** Yes, I think it is a little different. People can travel more now, and many people send New Year wishes online. However, spending time with family is still very important.

**Teacher:** Do you prefer traditional festivals or modern celebrations?  
**Me:** I prefer traditional festivals because they have more cultural meaning and they bring families together.

**Teacher:** What is the most important tradition during Tet?  
**Me:** For me, the most important tradition is the family reunion because it gives everyone a chance to meet and talk together.

## 13. Common mistakes to avoid

### Mistake 1 - `celebrate`

❌ We celebration Tet.  
✅ We **celebrate** Tet.

`celebrate` = verb  
`celebration` = noun

---

### Mistake 2 - `in Tet`

❌ In Tet, I visit my grandparents.  
✅ **During Tet**, I visit my grandparents.  
✅ **At Tet**, I visit my grandparents.

---

### Mistake 3 - past tense for a general tradition

If you are talking about what normally happens:

❌ People cleaned their houses before Tet.  
✅ People **clean** their houses before Tet.

Use past tense only if you are talking specifically about last year.

> Last Tet, we visited my hometown.

---

### Mistake 4 - `give lucky money for children`

❌ Adults give lucky money for children.  
✅ Adults give lucky money **to** children.

```text
give something to someone
```

---

### Mistake 5 - `very meaning`

❌ Tet is very meaning for me.  
✅ Tet is very **meaningful** to me.

---

### Mistake 6 - sentence too long

Avoid trying to connect everything with `and`.

Hard to speak:

> Tet is important and people clean houses and buy food and visit families and children get money and...

Better:

> Tet is very important in Vietnam. **Before Tet**, people clean their houses and prepare food. **During Tet**, families visit each other. Children also receive lucky money.

Use short sentences and clear connectors.

---

## 17. 5W1H method when you get stuck

If you forget what to say, think about:

```text
WHAT   -> What festival?
WHEN   -> When?
WHERE  -> Where?
WHO    -> Who celebrates with you?
WHY    -> Why is it important?
HOW    -> How do people celebrate?
```

Example:

```text
WHAT: Tet
WHEN: January / February
WHERE: Vietnam
WHO: family and relatives
WHY: reunion, new beginning
HOW: food, visits, lucky money, decorations
```

These six ideas are already enough for a 1-minute answer.

```text
Opinion

```
