## 3. Main sentence patterns

### A. Asking about the number of family members

**Question**

> How many people are there in your family?

**Answer**

> There are ___ people in my family.

Examples:

> There are four people in my family.

> There are five people in my family: my parents, my brother, my sister, and me.

### Natural expansion

Instead of stopping after one sentence:

> There are ___ people in my family. They are ___. We are quite close and we usually spend time together at the weekend.

---

### B. Asking where the family lives

**Question**

> Where does your family live?

**Answer**

> My family lives in ___.

or

> They live in ___.

Examples:

> My family lives in Hanoi.

> My parents live in my hometown, but I live in Hanoi.

Important:

- **My family lives ...** -> singular subject in normal English usage.
- **My parents live ...** -> plural subject.

## 4. Very important: `look like` vs `be like`

This is probably the most important distinction in today's lesson.

### What does he/she **look like**?

Meaning: **Ngoại hình của người đó như thế nào?**

Talk about physical appearance:

- tall / short
- slim / thin
- strong
- young / old
- long hair / short hair
- black hair / brown hair
- glasses

Examples:

> What does your father look like?

> He is tall and has short black hair.

> What does your mother look like?

> She is quite slim and she has long black hair.

### What is he/she **like**?

Meaning: **Tính cách của người đó như thế nào?**

Talk about personality:

- kind
- friendly
- patient
- funny
- quiet
- hardworking
- helpful
- easy-going
- strict
- caring

Examples:

> What is your father like?

> He is friendly, hardworking, and quite funny.

> What is your mother like?

> She is kind and very caring.

### Remember

```text
LOOK LIKE -> appearance
BE LIKE   -> personality
```

## 5. Useful grammar for describing people

### `be` + adjective

```text
He is tall.
She is friendly.
My father is hardworking.
My mother is very patient.
```

### `have/has` + noun

```text
I have short black hair.
He has short black hair.
She has long hair.
```

Be careful:

❌ She has a long black hair.  
✅ She has long black hair.

### `There is / There are`

One person / thing:

> There is one child in the family.

More than one:

> There are four people in my family.

## 7. A complete 45-60 second speaking answer

Do not memorize every word. Use this as a structure.

> There are **___ people** in my family. They are **________________**. My family lives in **________________**.  
> My father is **________________** and he has **________________**. He is **________________** and **________________**.  
> My mother is **________________**. She is a very **________________** and **________________** person.  
> We are quite close. We usually **________________** together when we have free time.

### Easier version

> There are ___ people in my family. They are ___. My family lives in ___. My father is ___. My mother is ___. We are very close.

Use the **easy version first**. Fluency is more important than trying to use difficult vocabulary.

## 9. Common mistakes to avoid

### Mistake 1

❌ My family **have** four people.  
✅ There **are** four people in my family.

### Mistake 2

❌ Where **do** your family live?  
✅ Where **does** your family live?

Or, more naturally:

✅ Where **do your parents** live?

### Mistake 3

❌ What does your father like?

This usually asks about **his preferences**.

> What does your father like?  
> He likes football.

For personality:

> What **is** your father like?  
> He is friendly and hardworking.

For appearance:

> What does your father **look like**?  
> He is tall and has short black hair.

### Mistake 4

❌ He is have short hair.  
✅ He has short hair.

### Mistake 5

❌ She very kind.  
✅ She **is** very kind.

## 11. Small-talk phrases that make answers sound natural

Use one of these instead of answering too mechanically:

- **Sure.** There are four people in my family.
- **Actually,** my parents live in my hometown.
- **I would say** my father is quite quiet but very friendly.
- **In terms of personality,** my mother is very patient.
- **As for my family,** we are quite close.
- **Most of the time,** we have dinner together.

Do not overuse them. One phrase is enough.

---

## 12. Useful bridge to work and interview small talk

The lesson is basic, but the same structures are useful when an interviewer or foreign colleague makes small talk before discussing work.

Examples:

> I live in Hanoi with my family.

> My hometown is __________, but I currently live and work in Hanoi.

> My family is quite small. There are ___ people in my family.

> We are close, although everyone is quite busy during the week.

The goal is not to give too much personal information. Give a short answer, then continue the conversation naturally.
